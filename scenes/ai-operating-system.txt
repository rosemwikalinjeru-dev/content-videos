# hook
Right now, the operating system of your business is your memory.

# head
Who's waiting on a quote. Who hasn't paid. Which job is on Thursday. It's all in your head.

# risk
So when you're sick, or busy, or asleep, the business forgets.

# idea
An A-I operating system sounds fancy. It's just this. One place where the business remembers.

# lists
Four lists. Your customers. Your jobs. Your money. And your follow-ups.

# move
Move each thing out of your head, and onto the board. One at a time.

# sim
Now watch a demo Monday. A lead comes in. It lands on the board, not in your memory.

# sim2
The quote goes out. The follow-up is set. Nobody had to remember anything.

# ai
And here's where A-I comes in. It reads the board, and tells you what needs you today.

# order
But the order matters. Board first. A-I second. It can't organize what isn't written down.

# start
Start with one list this week. The one you'd lose sleep over.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
