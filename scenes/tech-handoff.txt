# moment
It's Wednesday, nine in the morning. Your plumber pulls up to a job, and calls the office.

# before
The gate code is in an email. The photos are in someone's texts. And the office is on another call.

# cost
So he waits in the truck. Twenty minutes, on a job you're paying him for.

# new
The fix is one job card, filled in the moment the customer books.

# after
Priya books online. Her address, gate code, and photos land on one card. Your office checks it, and it goes to the plumber's phone.

# verdict
Your plumber should start working, not start calling.
