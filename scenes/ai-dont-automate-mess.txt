# hook
Automating a broken process doesn't fix it. It just breaks it faster.

# mess
Here's a demo. A new customer, and seven steps before they're booked. Two of them are done twice.

# slow
By hand, it's slow. But a person catches the mistakes.

# auto
Now automate it, exactly as it is. Same seven steps. Same two mistakes.

# sim
Watch ten customers go through. The mistakes don't stop. They just arrive faster.

# why
Because an automation does what it's told. Even when what it's told makes no sense.

# fix1
So fix the process first. For every step, ask, why do we do this?

# fix2
Cut the steps nobody needs. Seven becomes four.

# then
Then automate the four. And keep one spot where a person checks.

# sim2
Same ten customers. Four steps. Nothing typed twice. One check before it goes out.

# rule
Simplify first. Automate second. In that order, every time.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
