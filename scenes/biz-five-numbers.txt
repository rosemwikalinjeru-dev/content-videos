# hook
You can't fix what you don't count. Here are five numbers every owner should know.

# n1
One. How many new leads came in this week.

# n2
Two. How many of them actually booked.

# n3
Three. What your average job is worth.

# n4
Four. How many customers come back.

# n5
Five. How many days it takes you to get paid.

# sim
Here's why they matter. Watch one month move through all five.

# sim2
A hundred leads. Forty book. At three hundred dollars a job, that's twelve thousand.

# sim3
Now lift just one number. Fifty book. That's fifteen thousand. Same leads. Same price.

# sim4
Or get paid in ten days, not forty. Same money, a month sooner.

# where
Each number points to a different fix. Leads is marketing. Booked is follow-up. Paid is invoicing.

# start
You don't need software. A notebook, and five lines every Friday, will do.

# cta
Write them down every Friday. The weak one shows you what to fix. Follow for more.
