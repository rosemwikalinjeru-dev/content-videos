# hook
You can't fix what you don't count. Here are five numbers every owner should know.

# n1
One. How many new leads came in this week.

# n2
Two. How many of them actually booked.

# n3
Three. What your average job is worth.

# n4
Four. How many customers come back.

# n5
Five. How many days it takes you to get paid.

# cta
Write them down every Friday. The weak one shows you what to fix. Follow for more.
