# hook
You don't need a big A-I project. You need five small steps, in the right order.

# s1
Step one. Pick the one task your team complains about most.

# s2
Step two. Write down how it works today. Every step, even the messy ones.

# s3
Step three. Fix the process on paper, before you touch any tool.

# s4
Step four. Automate the boring part, and keep a person on the decisions.

# s5
Step five. Watch it for a month. Count the hours saved, and the mistakes caught.

# cta
Small, boring, and measured. That's how it actually works. Follow for one fix a week.
