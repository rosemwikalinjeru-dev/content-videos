# hook
Your cheapest customer is often your most expensive one.

# a
Customer A pays full price. One call, one visit, done.

# b
Customer B wants a discount. Then come five calls, two changes, and a late payment.

# flip
On paper, B still looks like income. Count your hours, and it flips.

# lesson
Price isn't just money. It's also a filter for who you work with.

# do
You don't have to drop them. Just stop discounting to win them.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
