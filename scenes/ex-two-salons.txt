# hook
Two salons. Same street, same prices. One of them is quietly pulling ahead.

# split
Salon A answers texts whenever someone's free. Salon B replies in a minute, every time, even at night.

# speed
First advantage, speed. The salon that answers first gets the first chance to book.

# accuracy
Second, fewer mistakes. Details get typed once, not three times.

# time
Third, your team's time. They stop chasing messages, and start doing the work people pay for.

# gap
None of it feels big on day one. But small gaps, every single day, add up.

# cta
So which salon are you? Follow for one fix like this every week.
