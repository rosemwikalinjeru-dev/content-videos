# moment
It's Thursday, ten in the morning. Your eleven o'clock client just didn't show up.

# before
Jess booked two weeks ago. Nobody reminded her. And that chair sat empty.

# cost
Two no-shows a week is two slots you can never sell again.

# new
The fix is a reminder that asks for a reply. Not just a reminder.

# after
The day before, Jess gets a text. She replies yes, or picks a new time. No reply? Your front desk calls her.

# verdict
Do this before you buy any fancy A-I. It's the easiest win in the building.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
