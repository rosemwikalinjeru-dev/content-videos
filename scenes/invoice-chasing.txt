# moment
It's the end of the month. You finished the job three weeks ago. You're still waiting to get paid.

# before
The invoice went out once. Nobody followed up, because asking for money feels awkward.

# cost
Your crew got paid. You didn't. That gap comes out of your pocket.

# new
The fix is polite reminders that go out on their own, with a pay link every time.

# after
Three days before it's due, Dan gets a friendly heads-up. On the due date, a pay link. A week late? It comes to you, to call him personally.

# verdict
Automate the reminder. Keep the hard conversation human.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
