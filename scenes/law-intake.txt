# moment
It's Monday morning. Your law firm missed six calls over the weekend. Nobody knows who they were.

# before
Some left voicemails. Most didn't. And the ones who did were never written down.

# cost
Every one of those calls was someone who needed a lawyer that weekend.

# new
The fix is an answering assistant that takes the details, and never gives legal advice.

# after
Carlos calls on Saturday. The assistant takes his name, number, and what happened. Monday, your intake team reviews it, and an attorney calls him back.

# verdict
Let it take the message. Never let it give the advice.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
