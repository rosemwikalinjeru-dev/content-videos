# moment
It's Wednesday. You signed a new bookkeeping client two weeks ago. You still don't have their bank statements.

# before
You've sent six emails. Each one asks for something different. And they've stopped opening them.

# cost
Two weeks of back and forth, before you've done any actual work.

# new
The fix is one checklist link, with every document they need in one place.

# after
Anna opens one link, and uploads what she has. Missing something? She gets a reminder for just that item. When it's complete, you get a ping to start.

# verdict
Ask once, clearly. Let the reminders do the chasing.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
