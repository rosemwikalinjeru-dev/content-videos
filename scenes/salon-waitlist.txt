# moment
It's Thursday, eight in the morning. Your two o'clock just canceled. And five people asked for that exact slot this week.

# before
They're on a sticky note. Nobody has time to call them all. So the chair sits empty.

# cost
A last-minute cancel is fine. An empty chair isn't.

# new
The fix is a waitlist that texts everyone at once, and gives the slot to the first yes.

# after
The cancel comes in. All five get a text. Leah replies first, and she's booked. Nobody replies? Your front desk gets a heads-up.

# verdict
Cancellations happen. Empty chairs don't have to.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
