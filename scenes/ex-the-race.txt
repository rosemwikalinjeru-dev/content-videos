# hook
Your competitor isn't better than you. They're just faster at the boring stuff.

# setup
A homeowner asks three roofers for a quote, on the same night.

# race
Roofer one replies the next afternoon. Roofer two, the next morning. Roofer three replies in two minutes, with a few quick questions.

# result
Guess who gets the first visit.

# lesson
Staying ahead isn't about the fanciest A-I. It's answering, following up, and showing up, every time, without relying on memory.

# cta
Be the fast one. Follow for one fix like this every week.
