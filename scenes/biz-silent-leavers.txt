# hook
Your unhappiest customers usually don't complain. They just don't come back.

# quiet
Picture ten customers who had a so-so visit. Maybe one tells you.

# gone
The rest say thanks, leave, and quietly book somewhere else.

# why
Complaining feels awkward. Leaving is easy.

# fix
So ask first. One short message after every visit. How did we do?

# then
And when someone says, not great, call them the same day.

# cta
A complaint is a second chance. Silence isn't. Follow for one business fix every week.
