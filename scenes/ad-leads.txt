# moment
It's seven at night. Someone fills out your Facebook ad form for a new backyard.

# before
The lead lands in a spreadsheet. Your crew lead sees it two days later. By then, she's hired someone else.

# cost
You paid for that lead. And then you let it go cold.

# new
The fix is a reply in the first few minutes, asking what you'd ask on the phone.

# after
Nina gets a text right away, with three quick questions. Ready to start? You get an alert to call her. Just looking? She gets your project photos instead.

# verdict
Speed wins the job. But you still make the call.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
