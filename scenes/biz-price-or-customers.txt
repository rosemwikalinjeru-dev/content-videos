# hook
Want more profit? You have two dials. Most owners only turn one.

# base
Here's a demo business. Ten jobs a week, at two hundred dollars each. Each job costs one-fifty to do.

# more
Dial one, more customers. Two more jobs a week adds a hundred dollars in profit. And more work.

# price
Dial two, price. Ten percent higher adds two hundred in profit. For the same work.

# sim
Now run it for a year. Dial one. About five thousand dollars more. And a hundred extra jobs to do.

# sim2
Dial two. About ten thousand more. And not one extra job.

# catch
The catch? Some customers may leave. So test it on new customers first.

# lose
How many customers could you lose, and still break even? In this demo, almost three in ten.

# how
So don't guess. Raise it for new customers, and watch what happens for a month.

# cta
Know both dials before you chase more work. Follow for one business fix every week.
