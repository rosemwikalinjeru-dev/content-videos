# hook
If every decision waits for you, your business moves at your speed.

# pile
Quotes, refunds, schedule changes, supplier questions. They all land on one desk. Yours.

# cost
So when you're on a job, or asleep, everything stops.

# sort
Sort them into three piles. Only you. Someone else, with a rule. And nobody at all.

# rule
A rule sounds like this. Refunds under fifty dollars, the front desk decides.

# result
Now the small stuff moves without you. And you keep the decisions that need you.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
