# moment
It's Sunday night. A homeowner fills out the quote form on your website.

# before
It lands in an inbox nobody checks until Tuesday. By then, Lisa has hired someone else.

# cost
Every slow reply is a quote you never got to send.

# new
The fix is simple. Reply in minutes, not days, even when you're off.

# after
Lisa gets a reply right away, with three quick questions. Her answers go into your customer list. If it's a real job, you get a text.

# verdict
Worth doing. Just keep a real person on the first phone call.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
