# hook
Sometimes the most profitable word in your business is, no.

# setup
It's a slow week, so you take a job that's a bad fit. Far away, low pay, vague details.

# block
It fills your Thursday. Then your ideal customer calls, and Thursday's gone.

# cost
The wrong job didn't just pay less. It cost you the right one.

# sim
Run a month both ways. Month one, you say yes to everything. Twenty jobs. Six of them bad fits.

# sim2
Those six eat your slots, your miles, and your patience.

# sim3
Month two, you turn those six down. Good jobs take some of their places. And you get your evenings back.

# fix
Write down three rules for a good job. Your area, your minimum price, your type of work.

# use
When a request breaks two of them, say no, and point them to someone else.

# how
Saying no politely is one sentence. That's not a fit for us, but here's someone who can help.

# fear
Scared of an empty calendar? Keep one flexible day a week for the maybe jobs.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
