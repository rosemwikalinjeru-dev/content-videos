# moment
It's Sunday morning. Your A-I receptionist just told a client she'll get a full refund.

# before
Jess asked about a refund. The A-I didn't know your policy, so it guessed. And it guessed yes.

# cost
Now your front desk starts Monday by taking back a promise.

# new
The fix is one rule. Money questions are never the A-I's call.

# after
When Jess asks for a refund, the A-I tells her a person will reply, and sends your front desk a summary. They decide, and Jess hears back.

# verdict
Let it book appointments. Keep refunds human.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
