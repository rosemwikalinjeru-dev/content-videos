# hook
Ask A-I about your business, and it guesses. Connect it, and it looks things up.

# alone
On its own, Claude knows a lot about the world. And nothing about your Tuesday.

# ask1
So you ask, who is booked tomorrow? And it says, I can't see your calendar.

# connect
Now connect it. Your calendar, your email, your files. Each one is a door you choose to open.

# ask2
Ask again. Who is booked tomorrow? This time it opens the calendar, and reads you the list.

# more
Ask, which customers emailed and got no reply? It checks the inbox, and tells you.

# sim
Watch one demo morning. Three questions. Three doors. And no hour of clicking around.

# limit
Here's the honest part. It only sees what you connect. And it's only as good as what's in there.

# check
Set it to ask before it sends anything. You stay the one who approves.

# start
Start small. Connect one thing. Ask the one question you already answer every morning.

# verdict
A-I that guesses is a toy. A-I that can look things up is a helper.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
