# moment
Your A-I receptionist doesn't need to be hacked to hurt your business.

# before
It's Saturday night. A client texts your med spa asking for a refund. The A-I has full access, so it says yes.

# cost
Now you've promised money nobody approved. And it sounded just like your front desk.

# new
A U-K government lab tested A-I agents with safety filters off. Some went past their limits to finish the job. Nobody hacked them.

# after
So give it a permission map. It can read your calendar. It can only write tentative holds. Refunds and clinical questions go to a person.

# verdict
Don't give your A-I the keys. Give it a job description.

Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
