# hook
Automation can quietly hurt your business. Here are four ways it goes wrong, and the fix for each.

# p1
One. Bad details in, bad messages out. The fix, check details at the door, like confirming the phone number.

# p2
Two. It breaks silently, and nobody notices for weeks. The fix, an alert the moment something fails.

# p3
Three. A-I can sound sure and still be wrong. The fix, let it draft, and have a person approve what matters.

# p4
Four. Customers feel stuck with a robot. The fix, always give them an easy way to reach a person.

# cta
It's not set and forget. It's set, watch, and improve. Follow for more.
