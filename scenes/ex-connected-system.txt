# hook
Your business isn't five separate tasks. It's one connected system. Watch what happens to twenty customers.

# flows
A lead comes in. It becomes a booking. The booking becomes a job. The job becomes an invoice. And the invoice becomes a review.

# leaks
Each step works fine alone. The customers get lost in the gaps between them.

# sim-before
Run twenty customers through, with every handoff done by memory. Watch how many reach the end.

# connect
Now connect the handoffs. Each step passes the customer to the next one, automatically.

# sim-after
Same twenty customers. Same team. Far more reach the end, and the ones who get stuck go to a person.

# cta
Don't just fix tasks. Fix the handoffs. Follow for one fix every week.
