# hook
A full calendar doesn't mean you're making money. Let's check three jobs.

# j1
Job one pays four hundred dollars. It takes two hours, ten minutes away.

# j2
Job two pays six hundred. But it's a full day, and an hour's drive each way.

# j3
Job three pays three hundred. It takes forty-five minutes, five minutes away.

# reveal
Per hour of your time, the smallest job wins. And the biggest one comes last.

# lesson
So stop asking how busy you are. Ask what each hour brings in.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
