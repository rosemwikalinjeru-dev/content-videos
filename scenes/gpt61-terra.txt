# hook | v3
OpenAI just released an A-I that uses a computer for you... [pause] at half the price.

# what
It's called G-P-T six point one Terra. It reads the screen, clicks, types, and fills in forms.

# price
One dollar per million input tokens, five per million output. Half the price of the model it replaces.

# speed
OpenAI says it's over three times faster on computer tasks. That's their own test, not an independent one.

# caveat
And it's not their smartest model.

# business
Where's that useful? Picture a dental clinic. Patient details arrive by email... and someone retypes them into an old system with no A-P-I.

# fix
That retyping is the job it's built for. It fills in the form... then a person checks it before saving.

# verdict
My verdict? Worth testing. Not worth trusting alone. A wrong date of birth costs more than the tokens you save.
