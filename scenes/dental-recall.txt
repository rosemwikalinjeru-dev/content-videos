# moment
It's Tuesday afternoon. Your hygienist has three empty slots. And hundreds of past patients are overdue.

# before
Nobody reminds them. They meant to book. Life got busy, and they just drifted away.

# cost
Every empty hygiene slot is time you've already paid for.

# new
The fix is a recall schedule that starts when they're due, and stops the moment they book.

# after
At six months, Maria gets a text with open times. A month later, a friendly nudge. Still nothing? Your front desk gives her a call.

# verdict
Your best new patients are the ones you already have.

# cta
Follow. I fix one business process like this every week.
