# hook
A-I doesn't know your prices, your area, or your rules. Until you write them down.

# problem
Ask it to answer a customer, and it sounds confident. And gets your business wrong.

# why
It isn't broken. Nobody told it how you work.

# p1
So give it five pages. Page one. What you do, and what you don't.

# p2
Page two. Your prices, or how you quote.

# p3
Page three. Where you work, and when.

# p4
Page four. Your rules. Deposits, cancellations, and what needs your okay.

# p5
Page five. How you talk to customers. With a few real examples.

# sim
Watch the same question, before and after. Do you work Saturdays?

# sim2
Before, a guess. After, it reads page three. Yes, until two. Here's how to book.

# bonus
And those five pages help every new hire too. Human or not.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
