# hook
Here's what happens when a heating and cooling company automates just one thing. Replying to new leads.

# d1
The reply goes out in a minute. So more people actually book.

# d2
A fuller schedule means techs aren't sitting around between jobs.

# d3
Fast service gets talked about. Ask at the right moment, and those customers leave reviews.

# d4
More reviews bring in more leads, and the whole cycle starts again.

# twist
But the ripple works both ways. Automate a broken process, and you just make mistakes faster.

# cta
Fix the process first. Then automate it. Follow for one fix every week.
