# hook
Three things I would not hand to A-I in a service business.

# one
One. The angry customer. They don't want a fast answer. They want a person.

# one2
A-I can tell you they're upset. A person should make the call.

# two
Two. The price exception. A discount, a refund, a favor for a regular.

# two2
That's your money and your judgment. A-I can prepare it. You approve it.

# three
Three. Anything where being wrong is expensive. Health. Legal. Safety.

# three2
There, A-I can take the message. It shouldn't give the answer.

# flip
So what does A-I get? The repeated stuff. Reminders. First replies. Sorting. Notes.

# sim
Watch one demo day. Twenty messages come in. Most are routine. A few need a person.

# sim2
A-I clears the routine. And puts the few in front of you, first.

# verdict
A-I for the routine. People for the moments that matter.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
