# hook
What happens to your business the day your best employee quits?

# head
She knows the supplier codes, the tricky customers, and how the schedule really works. None of it is written down.

# leave
When she leaves, all of that leaves with her.

# fix
The fix takes an hour a week. Pick one task, and have her write down the steps.

# test
Then test it. Can someone new follow those steps, without asking her?

# result
Ten weeks later, ten tasks live in the business. Not in one head.

# cta
Follow for one business fix every week.
