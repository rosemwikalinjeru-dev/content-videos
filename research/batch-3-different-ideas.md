## 1. review-requests — HVAC company (after-diagram: BRANCH)

### The one external fact: Google prohibits "review gating"
- Google's Maps / Business Profile **Prohibited & restricted content** policy, under *fake engagement*, disallows
  "discouraging or prohibiting negative reviews, or **selectively soliciting positive reviews from customers**."
- Review gating = asking "how did we do?" and sending only happy customers to the public review page.
- Compliant design: **everyone gets the review link**. A low score *additionally* triggers a call from the office.
  The call doesn't replace the link.

### Related, not used in the voiceover
- The FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials rule (16 CFR Part 465, effective Oct 21, 2024) bans fake reviews and
  some review-suppression practices. Sources differ on whether every gating setup violates it, so **the
  script says only "Google's rules"**, and doesn't say "illegal".
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-08-22/html/2024-18519.htm
