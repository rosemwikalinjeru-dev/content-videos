## Facts the scripts lean on

### Claude can connect to business tools (videos 1, 7)
- Anthropic's help center: Google Workspace connectors for **Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive**; "Ask Claude a
  question that requires access to your Gmail, Calendar, or Drive", and Claude can only access the data of the Google
  account you connected. On Team/Enterprise plans an owner must enable connectors first.
  https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors
- Connectors overview (first-party integrations, authenticate to use): https://claude.com/docs/connectors/overview.md
- "Connect your tools": https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11817150-connect-your-tools-to-unlock-a-smarter-more-capable-ai-companion

**Confirmed (search summary of the help page; the page itself was not opened from this environment):** connectors exist
for calendar, email and files; Claude only sees accounts you connect.

**★ Verify before posting (secondary sources only):** that sending email asks for approval by default. The script
therefore says "**Set it** to ask before it sends anything" (advice), not "it always asks" (a product claim).
Plan availability differs between sources; the scripts make **no claim about plans or price**.

**Not claimed:** speed ("in seconds"), accuracy, or that Claude connects to any specific CRM or invoicing tool.
Video 7 says "your tools have to be connected" without naming which ones.

### Chatbot vs automation vs agent (videos 2, 5)
- Anthropic, "Building effective agents": **workflows** are systems where models and tools are "orchestrated through
  predefined code paths"; **agents** "dynamically direct their own processes"; advice is to start simple.
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents
- Script wording in owner terms: automation = "follows a checklist, same steps every time"; agent = "decides the next
  step itself, and uses your tools". "Chatbot = answers questions" is the plain-language third category (Rose's framing).

### Rose's own method (videos 3, 4, 5, 6, 8)
Reasoning and advice, consistent with her operating principle "do not automate a broken process" and with the
permission-map episodes (Ep 06–08). No statistics.

## Video 9: "a competitor of yours already uses AI" (added 2026-11-13)

Rose asked for "how to make AI efficient in your business as your competitors are doing".

- Surveys of small-business AI use disagree widely because they define "using AI" differently:
  U.S. Census Bureau business survey (all firms, not only small): roughly 17-20% from Dec 2025 to May 2026;
  U.S. Chamber of Commerce 2025 report: almost 60% of small businesses; Thryv (Apr 2026): 66%; Goldman Sachs (Mar 2026): 76%.
  Sources: https://www.uschamber.com/assets/documents/Empowering-Small-Business-The-Impact-of-Technology-on-U.S.-Small-Business-Small-Business-Report-2026.pdf
  · https://www.uschamberfoundation.org/workforce/half-of-small-business-workers-use-ai-most-to-boost-productivity-not-automate-jobs
  (figures taken from search summaries; originals not opened from this environment).
- **So the script uses no number.** It says "Chances are, a competitor of yours already uses AI" (hedged) and never
  claims competitors are getting results. "A lot of AI use looks like this" is Rose's observation, not a statistic.
- The five steps are Rose's method: one task, written-down rules, connected tools, a person checking, count before and after
  (the measurement step is the brand differentiator).
- The two demo businesses are labelled DEMO; no outcome numbers.
