## Claims in the script

| Claim | Type | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Plumber is on a job and can't answer, so the caller tries the next plumber | Demo story | Labelled DEMO on screen. Customer "Mike" is fictional. |
| "Miss three calls like that a week, and that's three jobs gone" | **Demo number** | Illustrative, labelled DEMO. Not an industry statistic. |
| "When a call is missed, a text goes out within a minute" | Design of the fix | Missed-call text-back is a standard feature of most business phone systems and automation tools. No specific product or price is named. |
| "It saves jobs you already paid to win" | Reasoning | Ad spend and listings already brought the call in. Not a statistic. |

## No industry statistics used

I deliberately didn't use "X% of callers won't leave a voicemail"-type stats. I don't have a primary source for one, and the rules say nothing goes in without a source.
