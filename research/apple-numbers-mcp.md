**Video:** `apple-numbers-mcp` · **Format:** business teardown (Before → After through one customer), 35–45s

## What happened

- **Who:** Apple
- **When:** October 27, 2026 (Apple Newsroom)
- **What:** Keynote, Pages and Numbers get new AI features **in beta**: a built-in assistant (Apple models + OpenAI),
  and **Model Context Protocol (MCP) support**. Apple says MCP "enables external AI agents and tools to read
  content, perform edits, and execute automated actions directly in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers."
- **Requirements:** MCP needs **macOS 27 or later** (Mac only). The assistant needs an Apple Intelligence-capable
  device on iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 / macOS 27, US English only.
- **Timing:** reported as arriving in **November 2026**.
