# Reference style notes
Source: TikTok @pro.glitch ("Professor Glitch"), video 7707347441095331086, 4 min 10 s, 720×1280.
Topic: "How to build a team of AI employees in Claude Code". Sent by Rose on 2026-11-13 as "an example of a video you can follow".
The video file itself is not stored in the repo (someone else's work). These are notes on technique only.

## What it does (frame study, one frame every 4 s)
1. **Light paper look.** Cream background, charcoal text, one warm accent (terracotta). Soft, hand-drawn feel. No dark UI.
2. **Kicker + headline that changes every 8–12 s.** Tiny mono kicker ("EMPLOYEE 1", "HOW THEY HAND OFF"), then a 2–4 word bold
   headline with the key words in the accent colour ("CONTEXT **LOST**", "ONE PAGE **OUT**", "JUST **SAY IT**").
3. **A presenter character.** A cartoon narrator stands bottom-left the whole time, gesturing. Next to him a small dark
   caption pill shows 2–3 words with one highlighted.
4. **One illustrated world that keeps coming back.** Desks with a "brain" worker each (Researcher, Writer, Checker, You),
   joined by a shared `handoff/` folder. The same map returns 5+ times with something new marked on it (arrows, a rule
   board, a cost gauge). Flow is shown by objects physically moving between desks.
5. **Zoom in, zoom out.** Each desk gets its own close-up (what it reads, what it writes), then back to the whole map.
6. **Real screens mixed in.** Actual tool screenshots (prompt box, file tree, settings, schedule) sit inside the same frame,
   with the important line boxed in the accent colour.
7. **One example all the way through.** A single client ("Northside Dental") and one task (Monday reports).
8. **Structure:** promise → the common mistake → the rule → build it piece by piece → what goes wrong → cost → recap → CTA.
9. **Honest beats.** "The mistake", "where teams fall apart", "the honest part: cost".

## What we take from it (adapted, not copied)
- Light paper palette with Rose's own accent, kicker + two-tone headline, caption pill.
- A presenter figure for Rose (stylised, faceless or simple cartoon: no real photo).
- One illustrated business "world" per video that persists and gets annotated, with the customer/task physically moving
  through it. Close-ups, then back to the map.
- One named demo business per video, start to finish.
- Real screen recordings where Rose has them.

## What we don't take
- His character, colours, brain icons or layouts as-is. Rose's videos need their own recognisable look.
- The 4-minute length for Reels. Use ~60 s for shorts; the long structure suits the Saturday YouTube build.
