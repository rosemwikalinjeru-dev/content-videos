# Series: "Can I Automate This Business?"
Started: 2026-11-03 · Owner: Rose Njeru Automation · Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts (+ LinkedIn teardown posts)

## The promise (one line, used everywhere)
**Every week, I take one kind of service business, find where customers fall through the cracks, and show the fix:
what to automate, and where a person still steps in.**

## What makes an episode
- One business, one broken process, one named demo customer
- Before flow (red bottleneck) → the fix (HyperFrames demo) → after flow (with a human checkpoint) → honest verdict
- Industry illustration, "EP NN · CAN I AUTOMATE THIS BUSINESS?" badge on the opening scene, series card at the end
- 35–45s, the owner-first 7-scene structure from CLAUDE.md
- Honest verdicts: some episodes end with "worth it", some with "not alone", and some should end with "don't automate this"
