## Weekly production rhythm (keeps approval to ~15 min/day)
- **Mon:** Claude pulls news from the last 7 days and proposes 7 news + 14 evergreen scripts for the week.
- **Mon:** Rose approves or edits the scripts in one pass (the only manual step besides posting).

## Evergreen backlog (owner pain → 7-scene teardown), ranked by how often service businesses feel it
1. After-hours web chat / DMs go unanswered (salon, med spa)
2. Leads from Facebook/Instagram ads never get a call back (home services)
3. Estimate sent, no follow-up, job lost (roofing, HVAC)
4. Google review never requested after a 5-star job (any local service)
5. New patient intake forms filled on paper, retyped (dental, physio)
6. Quote requests with no qualifying questions waste site visits (remodeler, landscaper)
7. Past customers never reactivated (dental recall, HVAC tune-ups)
8. Invoice sent, payment chased by hand (agencies, contractors)
9. Job details lost between office and technician (dispatch, plumbing)
10. Appointment changes by text not reflected in the calendar (salon, clinic)
11. Weekly owner report built by hand (any business with a CRM)
12. Inbound calls with no record of who called or why (law firm, clinic)
13. Referral sent, nobody thanks or tracks it (real estate, agencies)
14. Waitlist kept on paper, cancellations go unfilled (med spa, salon)
15. Onboarding a new client takes 6 emails back and forth (agencies, bookkeepers)

## Guardrails
- 3/day is content, not sales. The revenue priority still comes first: prospecting and conversations before
  content work each day. Content should feed DMs and profile visits, not replace outreach.
- Every reel still follows CLAUDE.md: owner-first 7 scenes, demo numbers labelled, sources for news,
  AI-generated label on, and script approval before voice.
