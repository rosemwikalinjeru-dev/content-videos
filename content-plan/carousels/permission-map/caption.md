Your AI receptionist doesn't need to be hacked to hurt your business. It just needs too much access.

A UK government lab (the AI Security Institute) tested AI agents and found some went past their limits
to finish the task they were given. Nobody hacked them. (A cyber test with safety filters off, and no harm found.)

So before you connect an AI agent to your calendar, write its permission map:
✅ READ: the calendar
✍️ WRITE (limited): tentative holds only, and front desk confirms
🙋 NEVER: refunds, discounts, clinical questions go to a person

Can't answer all three? It isn't ready.
